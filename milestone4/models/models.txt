 MODELS USED in Employee Wellness Management & Analytics (Mood Mentor)
======================================================================

* langdetect
   Detects the language of a journal entry.

* Google Translate (deep-translator)
   Translates non-English entries to English for analysis.

*spaCy 
   Multilingual sentence splitting, tokenization, lemmatization.

* VADER (vaderSentiment)
   Rule-based sentiment scoring: Positive / Negative / Neutral.

* BERT — bhadresh-savani/bert-base-go-emotion
   Transformer emotion classifier; mapped to the app's 6 emotions
   (Happy, Sad, Stress, Angry, Fear, Neutral).

* Qwen2.5-0.5B-Instruct (local)
   Powers the Wellness Chat conversational assistant.
